Understand the risks

Borrowing has responsibilities.

Your collateral supports your loan. If its market value falls or your debt grows enough to cross the liquidation threshold, some collateral may be sold to repay the debt. Review market liquidity, oracle pricing, interest rates, and liquidation limits before using a lending protocol.
