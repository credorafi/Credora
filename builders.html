Credora builders

Build on the markets, keep half the fee.

An app that routes deposits or loans through Credora can earn a share of the performance fee on the volume it brings. Attribution is designed to be recorded on chain, with public accounting rather than invoices to chase.

1 — Take an address

Register your address

Register a payout address to identify your integration when it routes transactions.

2 — Route volume

Integrate the markets

Use the SDK or supported contract calls to route supply and borrow activity with attribution.

3 — Get paid

Claim eligible fees

Eligible rewards require a configured accounting and distribution system before claims can work.

Attribution

A builder attribution tag can be carried alongside a transaction so an indexer can identify the address associated with routed volume. The exact encoding must match Credora's deployed contracts and transaction integration.

An attribution tag by itself is not proof that a reward is owed. Eligibility, registered addresses, accounting rules and payouts must be verified against the actual protocol implementation.

Free flash loans

Flash loans allow an asset to be borrowed and returned within the same transaction, subject to the lending protocol's rules. They can be used by liquidators, arbitrage strategies and transaction routers.

To enable this feature, integrate the verified lending contract and its actual flashLoan(token, assets, data) interface. Do not submit transactions until the network, contract address and callback requirements have been verified.

An MCP server

Model Context Protocol (MCP) can expose approved market data and protocol actions to compatible AI agents. A production integration needs a configured MCP endpoint and documented tools, permissions and transaction-signing flow.

This page does not claim that a live Credora MCP server is available until the endpoint has been deployed and verified.
