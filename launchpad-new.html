The launch process

What a launch will do

Two signatures: Pons mints the coin for 0.0005 ETH, then the launchpad registers it. The second one is what makes it a Cluby coin — until it lands, the coin exists and its tax has nowhere to go.

01

1 · Pons mints the coin.

A fixed supply on a Pons curve, paired against USDG, with the trading tax you choose below. The launch names a wallet this protocol controls as the tax recipient — Pons fixes that at launch and nobody, including us, can change it afterwards.

02

2 · The coin registers.

Permissionless and checked: the pair must belong to the coin, must already be paying that wallet, and the market must be one Cluby has listed whose loan token is what the coin trades against.

03

3 · The tax splits.

Every trade pays 5%, fixed by Pons at launch and changeable afterwards by nobody, plus Pons's own 1% pair fee — 6% in all. The selected tax in the form above is only a frontend preview until the actual launch integration is connected.

04

4 · The interest burns it.

Each harvest takes only what the position earned, buys the coin on its own curve and destroys it.

Fair launch

What you keep

Nothing is allocated to whoever launches. There is no creator share of the tax and no reserved supply — the launcher buys on the same curve as everyone else, at the same price, in the same block. What a launch gets you is a coin that lends.

✓
No creator tax share

Launching a coin does not allocate a share of the trading tax to its creator.

✓
No reserved supply

There is no supply reserved specifically for the launcher.

✓
Same curve for everyone

The launcher buys on the same curve as everyone else, at the same price, in the same block.

✓
A coin that lends

The launch creates the opportunity for a coin to lend into its selected market after registration.
